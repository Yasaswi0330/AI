Text Splitters:
> Text Splitters are utilities in langchain to break large content into smaller chunks.

> LLMs has token limit (Max token per request). To process long documents, we must split into manageable pieces

Large Document -> Text Splitter -> Smaller chunks -> LLM or Vector Database

"RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter"

Parameters

chunk_size: Maximum characters per chunk

chunk_overlap: No of characters overlapping between chunks.
